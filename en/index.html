About us

FjordPets is a calm and professional dog grooming salon where the dog’s comfort and wellbeing always come first. The salon is run by a trained dog groomer with experience from both Lithuania and Norway. After several years of working in other fields, the decision was made to follow a strong passion for animals. Since then, the focus has been on developing skills in dog grooming, care and styling. Through salon experience and continuous professional development, the goal is to offer gentle, safe and individually adapted treatments for every dog. At FjordPets, patience, calm handling and quality are important in every part of the visit. The goal is that both dogs and owners feel safe, cared for and happy after each appointment.
