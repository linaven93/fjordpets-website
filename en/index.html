About us

FjordPets is a calm and professional dog grooming salon where the dog’s comfort and wellbeing always come first. Behind the salon is Ausra Maciene, a trained dog groomer with experience from both Lithuania and Norway, and a strong passion for animals and coat care. After several years in administration and management, she chose to follow her dream of working with dogs full time. Through education, courses and practical experience, she has built FjordPets with focus on quality, safety and personal care. At FjordPets, every dog receives individual treatment adapted to coat, needs and temperament. The goal is to create a safe and stress-free experience where both dog and owner feel cared for.
